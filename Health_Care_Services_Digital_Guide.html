1. Basic Information

What are Health Care Services?

Health care services are services provided to protect, improve or restore a person’s health. They may include prevention, diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, surgery, rehabilitation, mental health support and patient education.

Στα ελληνικά: Οι υπηρεσίες υγείας είναι οι υπηρεσίες που παρέχονται για την πρόληψη, τη διάγνωση, τη θεραπεία και την αποκατάσταση της υγείας ενός ατόμου. Μπορεί να παρέχονται σε νοσοκομεία, κέντρα υγείας, ιατρεία, εργαστήρια, μονάδες αποκατάστασης ή ακόμη και στο σπίτι.

Prevention

Vaccination, screening, health education and regular check-ups.

Πρόληψη: εμβολιασμοί, προληπτικός έλεγχος, ενημέρωση και τακτικές εξετάσεις.

Diagnosis

Finding out what disease, injury or condition a patient has.

Διάγνωση: εντοπισμός της πάθησης ή του προβλήματος υγείας.

Treatment

Medication, surgery, therapy or other medical care used to help a patient recover.

Θεραπεία: φάρμακα, χειρουργική επέμβαση, θεραπευτικές παρεμβάσεις.

Rehabilitation

Helping a patient regain movement, strength, independence and quality of life.

Αποκατάσταση: επανάκτηση κίνησης, δύναμης και λειτουργικότητας.

3. Who provides the services?

Health Care Professionals

Doctor / Physician

Examines patients, makes a diagnosis and recommends treatment.

Γιατρός: εξετάζει τον ασθενή, κάνει διάγνωση και προτείνει θεραπεία.

Nurse

Provides direct patient care, gives medication, checks vital signs and dresses wounds.

Νοσηλευτής/τρια: παρέχει άμεση φροντίδα, χορηγεί φάρμακα, μετρά ζωτικά σημεία και περιποιείται τραύματα.

Paramedic

Provides emergency care before and during transport to hospital.

Διασώστης/τρια: παρέχει επείγουσα προνοσοκομειακή φροντίδα.

Radiographer / Radiologic technologist

Performs medical imaging examinations.

Τεχνολόγος ακτινολογίας: πραγματοποιεί εξετάσεις ιατρικής απεικόνισης.

Physiotherapist

Uses exercise, movement and physical techniques to improve mobility and function.

Φυσικοθεραπευτής/τρια: βοηθά στη βελτίωση της κίνησης και της λειτουργικότητας.

Laboratory technician

Analyses blood, tissue and other specimens.

Εργαστηριακός τεχνικός: αναλύει αίμα και άλλα βιολογικά δείγματα.

Pharmacist

Dispenses medication and explains safe use.

Φαρμακοποιός: εκτελεί συνταγές και δίνει οδηγίες για σωστή χρήση φαρμάκων.

Psychologist / Therapist

Supports mental health and helps patients manage emotional or behavioural difficulties.

Ψυχολόγος / θεραπευτής: παρέχει υποστήριξη για ζητήματα ψυχικής υγείας.
4. Inside the Hospital

Major Hospital Departments

Accident & Emergency (A&E) / Casualty Department — Τμήμα Επειγόντων Περιστατικών

A medical treatment facility for patients who need urgent or emergency care and often arrive without an appointment, either on their own or by ambulance. It treats a wide range of illnesses and injuries, including life-threatening conditions. It normally operates 24 hours a day.

Example: The patient was taken to the A&E department after a serious road accident.
General Surgery / Endoscopic Surgery — Γενική Χειρουργική / Ενδοσκοπική Χειρουργική

Surgery uses operative techniques to investigate or treat disease, injury or other conditions. It may repair damaged tissue, remove diseased tissue or improve bodily function.

Η χειρουργική χρησιμοποιεί επεμβατικές τεχνικές για διάγνωση ή θεραπεία παθήσεων και τραυματισμών.
Anaesthetics — Αναισθησιολογικό

Anaesthesia allows medical procedures to be carried out without pain.

General anaesthesia unconsciousness and complete loss of sensation.

Sedation reduces anxiety and awareness without necessarily causing unconsciousness.

Regional / local anaesthesia blocks sensation in a specific part of the body while the patient remains conscious.

Cardiology — Καρδιολογικό

Deals with disorders of the heart and circulatory system, including heart failure and congenital heart defects.

Example: The cardiologist checked the patient’s pulse and blood pressure.
Diagnostic Imaging / Radiology — Ακτινολογικό / Ιατρική Απεικόνιση

Uses imaging methods to create pictures of the inside of the body for diagnosis and medical intervention.

X-rayUltrasoundCTMRIPETNuclear medicine

Χρησιμοποιεί τεχνικές όπως ακτινογραφία, υπέρηχο, αξονική και μαγνητική τομογραφία.
Obstetrics & Gynaecology — Μαιευτική και Γυναικολογία

Obstetrics covers pregnancy, childbirth and the postpartum period. Gynaecology focuses on the health of the female reproductive system.

Haematology — Αιματολογικό

Deals with blood diseases and may include a laboratory for haematological tests.

Example: A blood sample was sent to the haematology laboratory.
Microbiology — Μικροβιολογικό

A diagnostic laboratory dealing with bacteriology, mycology, parasitology, virology and mycobacteriology. It receives specimens from both in-patients and out-patients.

Paediatrics — Παιδιατρικό

Provides medical care for infants, children and adolescents.

Physiotherapy (PT) — Φυσικοθεραπεία

Helps improve mobility, function and quality of life through examination, diagnosis, exercise therapy, manual therapy, movement and patient education.

Orthopaedics — Ορθοπαιδικό

Deals with the musculoskeletal system: bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, muscles and nerves. Treatment may include medication, exercise, casting, surgery and rehabilitation.

Neurology & Psychiatry — Νευρολογικό και Ψυχιατρικό

Neurology treats conditions involving the central and peripheral nervous systems. Psychiatry focuses on the diagnosis, prevention and treatment of mental disorders and difficulties involving mood, behaviour, cognition and perception.

5. Practical Health Care English

Common Procedures and Actions

Dressing a wound

Περιποίηση / αλλαγή επιδέσμου σε τραύμα.
The nurse is dressing the patient’s wound after cleaning it.

Using a stethoscope

Χρήση στηθοσκοπίου.
The doctor is using a stethoscope to listen to the patient’s lungs.

Talking to a therapist

Συζήτηση με θεραπευτή / ψυχολόγο.
The patient is talking to a therapist about stress and anxiety.

Doing an abdominal ultrasound

Διενέργεια υπερήχου κοιλίας.
The radiologist is doing an abdominal ultrasound to examine the liver.

Preparing a prescription

Σύνταξη / προετοιμασία ιατρικής συνταγής.
The doctor is preparing a prescription for antibiotics.

Measuring pulse rate

Μέτρηση σφυγμών / καρδιακού ρυθμού.
The nurse is measuring the patient’s pulse rate.

Examining a melanoma

Εξέταση μελανώματος / ύποπτης δερματικής βλάβης.
The dermatologist is examining a melanoma carefully.

Getting corneas checked

Έλεγχος κερατοειδούς χιτώνα.
The patient is getting her corneas checked by an eye specialist.

Measuring blood glucose level

Μέτρηση επιπέδου γλυκόζης / σακχάρου στο αίμα.
The nurse is measuring the patient’s blood glucose level.

Using a walker

Χρήση περιπατητήρα / “Π”.
The patient is using a walker during rehabilitation.

Giving an injection

Χορήγηση ένεσης.
The nurse is giving the patient an injection.

Checking blood pressure

Μέτρηση αρτηριακής πίεσης.
The nurse is checking the patient’s blood pressure.

7. Communication at Work

Useful English for Health Care Settings

Questions to a patient

• How are you feeling today?

• Where does it hurt?

• How long have you had these symptoms?

• Are you allergic to any medication?

• Are you taking any medication at the moment?

• Have you had this problem before?

Instructions to a patient

• Please sit down and relax.

• Roll up your sleeve, please.

• Take a deep breath.

• Please lie on your back.

• Try not to move.

• Take this medication twice a day.

Professional tip: In health care English, clear and polite language is essential. Use simple instructions, check that the patient understands, and avoid unnecessary technical terms when speaking to patients.
8. Group Work

Create a Mind Map: Health Care Services

Work in groups. You may use English and Greek. Try to include as much relevant information as possible.

A. What services are provided?

Emergency care, diagnosis, surgery, imaging, laboratory tests, medication, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, maternity care, paediatric care, mental health support, health education…

B. Who provides them?

Doctors, nurses, paramedics, radiographers, laboratory technicians, physiotherapists, pharmacists, psychologists, therapists and other health professionals.

C. Where and with what facilities?

Hospitals, clinics, health centres, laboratories, imaging centres, rehabilitation units, ambulances and home care. Think about equipment such as scanners, monitors, hospital beds, wheelchairs, walkers and laboratory equipment.

Challenge: Add one branch called “Patient journey” and show what may happen from admission → diagnosis → treatment → rehabilitation → discharge.
9. Self-check

Can you use the vocabulary?

1. Which department usually treats a patient with a broken leg?

Orthopaedics.

2. Which department uses MRI, CT and ultrasound?

Diagnostic Imaging / Radiology.

3. What is the difference between an in-patient and an out-patient?

An in-patient is admitted to hospital and stays there; an out-patient receives care without staying overnight.

4. Complete: “The nurse is ______ the patient’s blood pressure.”

checking / measuring

5. Why is physiotherapy important after some injuries or operations?

It helps restore movement, strength, function and independence.

Study Tip

Learn health care vocabulary in groups of meaning, not as isolated words. For example:

Emergency ambulance • A&E • injury • urgent • life-threatening

Diagnosis examination • blood test • specimen • X-ray • ultrasound • CT • MRI

Treatment medication • injection • surgery • prescription • physiotherapy • rehabilitation
