Your turn · Group project

Pitch your own healthcare organisation

Work in teams of 3–4. You are the planning committee for a new community health centre. Use the model ideas as inspiration, but make your own decisions based on the needs of your community.

  1. Choose a name for your centre and describe the people it will serve.
  2. Create your own spidergram, poster or digital presentation covering all five topics.
  3. Rank the five priorities from 1 (highest) to 5 (lowest). Explain your top three choices and discuss a trade-off if the budget is limited.
  4. Include at least five healthcare terms and four relative clauses, including one with where and one with who or that.
  5. Present your proposal in English (3–5 minutes). Every team member should speak. Answer one question from the class.
Opening: Our proposal is a community health centre which will serve ...
Prioritising: We believe that ... should be our first priority because ...
Concluding: In conclusion, our centre will be a place where ...

Language & Glossary

Useful language for discussion

In our opinion ... · We should prioritise ... · This is essential because ... · I agree, but ... · If we had to choose ... · The main advantage is ...

Relative clauses in context

A nurse who has emergency training. A device which/that measures blood pressure. A room where patients can be examined. A patient whose condition requires monitoring. A time when demand is high. The reason why we need more staff.

Use commas for extra, non-essential information: The centre, which will open next year, will serve local residents. Do not use that in non-defining clauses.
