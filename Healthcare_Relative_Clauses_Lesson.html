Learning goals

By the end of the lesson, you can choose the correct relative word, distinguish defining from non-defining clauses, and describe healthcare professionals, places and devices using accurate English.

Warm-up: What do you call a doctor who treats heart disease? Where do patients go when they need emergency care?

02 · Medical specialties

Who treats whom? Notice how relative clauses describe professionals and their patients.

03 · Hospital departments

Where does care take place? Use where for location and which when the place is the subject or object.

04 · Basic medical equipment

What does each device do? Use which / that to define its function.

06 · Pair work: hospital information desk

Student A: You are a new hospital volunteer. Ask about a specialist, a department and a device. Student B: Explain each using a relative clause. Swap roles.

Useful starters: A … is a person who … / The … is a place where … / A … is a device which … / A patient whose …

Exit ticket: Write one defining and one non-defining relative clause about healthcare. Underline the relative word and explain your comma choice.
